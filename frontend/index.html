Conservation

Technology in Service of the Wild.

AI will not protect a forest on its own. It can help the people who do, by making observation faster, wider and easier to share.

  1. 01

    Observe biodiversity

    Turn thousands of camera-trap images and field recordings into a clear picture of which species live where.

  2. 02

    Understand animal behavior

    Follow how animals feed, rest and move through a landscape across seasons, not just in single sightings.

  3. 03

    Support conservation decisions

    Give rangers, researchers and planners evidence they can question, verify and act on.

WildGuard AI is a research and monitoring aid. Its outputs should be reviewed by qualified people before informing any decision.
